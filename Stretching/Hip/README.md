# Hip

A 20-level hip-mobility path. Work on one level at a time. A level is ready to unlock when you can complete it with controlled movement, no sharp pain, and no symptom flare later that day or the next day.

Do not force range. Stop for sharp or increasing pain, numbness, tingling, loss of control, or symptoms after a fall. If hip pain affects sleep, walking, or normal activities, seek advice from a clinician or physiotherapist before progressing.

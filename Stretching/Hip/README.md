# Hip Mobility for Muay Thai

A 12-level path for improving usable hip rotation, range, and control for Muay Thai. Work on one level at a time and unlock the next only when the current level is controlled, free from sharp pain or pinching, and does not cause a symptom flare later that day or the next day.

## Progression rules

Progress by improving control first, then reducing support, increasing a comfortable range, slowing the movement, and finally increasing kicking height. Do not progress by forcing range or simply adding repetitions.

## Safety

Stop or reduce the range for sharp pain, joint pinching, numbness, tingling, loss of control, or symptoms that are clearly worse later that day or the next morning. Seek medical or physiotherapy advice if pain affects walking, sleep, or normal activities.

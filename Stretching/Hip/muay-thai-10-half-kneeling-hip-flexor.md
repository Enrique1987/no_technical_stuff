# Level 10 — Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Mobility

Use a cushion under the rear knee and hold a support. Tuck the pelvis slightly, make 6 gentle forward rocks, then hold a mild stretch without arching the lower back.

**Start:** 6 rocks plus a 20-second hold per side.

**Unlock next level:** Maintain a tall torso and mild stretch without front-of-hip pinching.

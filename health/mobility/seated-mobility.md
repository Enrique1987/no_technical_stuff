# Seated Mobility

Captured from personal mobility notes on October 6, 2026. The numerical scores below are subjective priorities from the supplied notes; they are not validated health-effectiveness measurements.

A collection of high-value mobility exercises that can be performed while sitting.

Useful during meetings, talks, lectures, sauna sessions, travel, or any other situation where you need to remain seated for several minutes.

## Selection Rule

Only exercises rated **7/10 or higher for General Health** are included.

The goal is not aggressive stretching. The goal is to use otherwise inactive sitting time to maintain or improve:

- Hip mobility
- Ankle mobility
- Pelvic movement
- Shoulder mobility
- Scapular control
- Neck mobility
- Posture

## Hip Internal Rotation — Important Technique Note

Sit with your knees bent at approximately 90°.

Keep the knees relatively close together and stable.

Move the feet outward while the knees remain approximately in place.

This creates **internal rotation of the hip**.

Do **not** intentionally turn the toes inward like a skiing snowplow.

The feet and ankles should remain relaxed and approximately neutral. The movement should come from the hip rather than from twisting the ankle.

A useful cue is:

> **Knees stay → feet move outward → hips rotate internally.**

You can also slightly lift the feet from the floor if this makes the movement easier and prevents friction from limiting the range.

## Simple Seated Mobility Circuit

When sitting for 10 minutes or more:

1. Figure 4
2. Hip internal rotation
3. Active knee opening
4. Ankle dorsiflexion
5. Ankle circles
6. Pelvic tilts
7. Scapular retraction
8. Shoulder circles
9. Neck rotation
10. Chin tuck

Spend approximately **30–60 seconds per movement**.

There is no need to perform the entire circuit every time. The main objective is to regularly change position and move joints that would otherwise remain static.

## Priority for Muay Thai

For improving hip mobility and kicking mechanics, prioritize:

1. **Figure 4 — 9/10**
2. **Hip Internal Rotation — 9/10**
3. **Active Knee Opening — 8/10**
4. **Scapular Retraction — 8/10**
5. **Ankle Dorsiflexion — 7/10**

The first three are especially useful for developing better hip movement while accumulating mobility work during otherwise inactive sitting time.

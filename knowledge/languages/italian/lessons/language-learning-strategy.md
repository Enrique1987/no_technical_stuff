## Expressions already used well

The original lesson marked these as correct or readily understandable:

- **Questo è proprio perfetto.** — This is just perfect.
- **Mi piace tantissimo.** — I like it very much.
- **Lo voglio provare.** — I want to try it.
- **Non lo so, vediamo.** — I do not know; let us see.
- **Devo andare al lavoro.** — I have to go to work.
- **Grazie mille per la lezione di oggi.** — Thank you very much for today's lesson.
- **Ci vediamo.** — See you.
- **Circolo virtuoso.** — Virtuous circle.
- **Tre volte alla settimana.** — Three times a week.
- **È per questo che lo voglio provare.** — That is why I want to try it.

## Five sentences for review

1. **Penso che questo piano abbia senso.**
2. **Voglio migliorare il mio italiano senza dedicarci troppo tempo.**
3. **Se noto piccoli progressi, mi viene voglia di continuare.**
4. **Il mio obiettivo è identificare le frasi più utili per comunicare.**
5. **Vorrei provare a studiare russo per dieci minuti, tre volte alla settimana.**

## Learning intentions discussed

- **Italian:** keep speaking regularly and receive corrections with concrete examples.
- **German and English:** maintain and refine languages already used in daily life and at work.
- **Russian:** consider an experiment of ten minutes, three times a week, initially focused on listening, pronunciation and useful phrases, without requiring written grammar or the alphabet from the start.
- **Portuguese:** a language to consider adding later.
- **80/20 approach:** look for words and structures with practical communication value. Frequency, usefulness in real situations and the ability to combine phrases were proposed selection criteria. No universal optimal 20% was established.
- **Motivation:** small results may encourage further practice, creating a virtuous circle. This was the working idea discussed, rather than a measured result.

Five to six new phrases per week gives approximately **260–312 phrases over 52 weeks**. The original note corrected a comparison with 1,000 phrases. Review and flexible combination matter alongside the number memorised.

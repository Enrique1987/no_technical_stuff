# Cooking and daily life

Personal Italian lesson from October 15, 2026, covering Bologna, ragù, cooking for the family, kefir and the school run.

The recorded attempts below were simplified in the original lesson note to remove hesitations and repetitions. Some wording may be approximate. Several alternatives improve naturalness rather than correcting an error. Food statements and cooking times are conversation examples, rather than nutritional guidance or a verified recipe.

## Practice focus

Practise prepositions and articles (*a scuola*, *nei ristoranti*, *per i bambini*), verb forms (*dici*, *compro*, *rimane*) and useful patterns (*ho sentito dire*, *ci vuole*, *dopo aver accompagnato*).

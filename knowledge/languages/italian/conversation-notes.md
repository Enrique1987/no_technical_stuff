## Corrections and model phrases recovered from the available exchanges

These are corrections or model sentences present in the recovered exchanges. They are separate from the complete lesson documents linked above.

| Date | Italian | English meaning or learning point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October 13 | **Su una sedia a rotelle.** | In a wheelchair; the vocabulary supplied during the conversation. |
| October 15 | **Difesa.** | Defence; corrected from the Spanish-influenced *difensa*. |
| October 20 | **Parliamo di cose semplici; per ora mi basta raccontare la mia giornata.** | Let us talk about simple things; for now, telling you about my day is enough. |
| October 21 | **Esiste un romanzo in cui gli Uomini Lucertola sono i protagonisti?** | Is there a novel in which the Lizardmen are the protagonists? Practise *un romanzo in cui...*. |
| October 22 | **Prima programmavo, adesso coordino.** | I used to program; now I coordinate. Contrast the imperfect with the present. |
| October 22 | **Consiglio il cliente sulle buone pratiche.** | I advise the client on good practices. |
| October 22 | **Ci sono statistiche al riguardo?** | Are there statistics on this? |
| October 22 | **Adesso vado a studiare le altre lingue.** | Now I am going to study the other languages. Use *le altre lingue*, rather than *gli altri lingue*. |
| October 26 | **Aspetta, devo controllare il mio tempo.** | Wait, I need to check my available time; the model sentence offered in the conversation. |
| November 3 | **Sono andato dall'oculista per sapere se devo fare un'operazione.** | Use *essere* with *andare* in the past tense, and *dall'oculista*. This is a language example. |
| November 3 | **Voglio fare l'operazione laser per non portare più gli occhiali.** | *Gli occhiali* means glasses; *gli occhi* means eyes. This records a practice sentence, rather than medical guidance. |
| November 3 | **Ma questa è la vita, devo fare troppe cose.** | *Cose* is feminine plural: *troppe cose*. |
| November 3 | **Fare la spesa e preparare la cena, tutto questo.** | *Tutto questo* refers to the whole set of activities, rather than people. |

# Savoury food ideas

This is the starting point for savoury dishes from the saved [fit-food video collection](../fit-food/videos/). The collection name does not guarantee that a dish is vegetarian or that a nutrition claim is accurate. Keep the source MP4s in their original collection; put a tested personal recipe in `food/` when ingredients and quantities are verified.
